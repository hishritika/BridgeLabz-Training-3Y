The Pink City

Jaipur is one of India's most beautiful and historic cities. Known as the Pink City, it is famous for its magnificent palaces, forts, colourful markets and rich culture.

Jaipur has a vibrant culture filled with traditional music, dance, handicrafts, festivals and delicious Rajasthani cuisine.

The city's colourful streets and historic architecture reflect the rich heritage of Rajasthan.
