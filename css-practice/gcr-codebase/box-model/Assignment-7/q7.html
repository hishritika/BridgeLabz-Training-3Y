The Power of Good Typography

Good typography makes information easier to read, understand and remember.

Typography is one of the most important parts of web design. A carefully selected font can improve the appearance of a webpage while also making its content easier to understand.

Different font families create different visual impressions. A serif font can provide a traditional and elegant appearance, while a sans-serif font often feels modern and clean.

Font size and weight also affect readability. Important information can be highlighted using bold text, while emphasis can be created with italic text.

"Typography is the voice of written communication."

Good spacing is equally important. Proper line-height and letter-spacing prevent text from looking crowded and help readers move comfortably from one line to the next.

When typography is balanced with sufficient whitespace, appropriate font sizes and readable line lengths, the overall design becomes clear, professional and accessible.
