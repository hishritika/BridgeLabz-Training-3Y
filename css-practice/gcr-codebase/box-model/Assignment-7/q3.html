Exploring Linear and Radial Gradients

Linear Gradient

A linear gradient creates a smooth transition between two or more colors along a straight line.

linear-gradient(135deg, #2563eb, #9333ea)

Radial Gradient

A radial gradient creates a circular or elliptical transition that spreads outward from a central point.

radial-gradient(circle, #f59e0b, #dc2626)
